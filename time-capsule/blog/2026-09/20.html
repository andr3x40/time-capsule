Doing a Blog in 2026

2026-09-20

Exactly. You're seeing a blog in 2026, like the good old days. It's for the better, trust me.
This is a post for the future where I explain why I'm doing a blog in recent times.

Here, I'll post about stuff I may do, like me discovering music, videogames, comics, books, you name it. I could talk about anything.
There's a button at the top of the page to subscribe to the RSS feed of my blog, if you want to.

it's actually an atom feed but it's basically the same as rss

But... who's going to see this blog? I mean, it's not like Instagram or Twitter. There's no feed for anybody to use to read these posts.
Well, it's something I can easily pass on. Nothing really changes between a blog that goes nowhere and posts that can go anywhere.
Also, I never used social media that much. I tried, but there's no platform that works for me.

My relationship with social media

The first time I used some sort of social media was back in 2016, when I made an Instagram account. There, I was sharing my gaming adventures, like my Minecraft builds.
Basically, I never used it in the way it was intended, which was sharing photos of your life. And I never wanted to.
Also, the Stories thing never clicked with me. Same goes for the new Notes thing, and now I keep Instagram just for the reels. There's good memes there.

In ten years, I used lots of social media platforms to see what was the best choice for me, and every time I found something I didn't like:

  • Instagram requires me to have a photo for each post and many times I have none to share, also what I write simply acts as a description
  • Instagram's stories last only for a day and they still don't appeal me after all the changes they did throughout the years
  • Twitter, Threads and Bluesky limit me on the amount of words I want to use, I don't like the thing of chaining individual posts and I certainly don't like how their discovery algorithms work
  • Reddit simply doesn't work that well for what I want to do
  • TikTok is the bottom of the barrel, that place sucks
  • BeReal was cool, but it fell down the "I want to look good" path Instagram has always taken
  • Mastodon and fediverse platforms have the same problem of every microblogging platform, but I could switch there
  • Facebook is a dead place full of genAI slop I want to burn down to ashes
The only one I never tried is Tumblr, which could do the job perfectly... but now it's more used for art. It could work, but... I don't know, it seems dead and alive at the same time, like Threads.
One day I'll try it anyway.

How I see social media

I never liked social media, but I don't mind using them. In 2016 it was a new thing for me, but after some time I began to see the mechanisms these places worked with. Especially Twitter, that place was, is and will always be awful. Same goes for Threads.

Every single social media platform has some sort of rewarding mechanism to keep you engaged with, but I don't care about numbers so it never really affected me. Or, at least, I care knowing that the number of likes, comments or shares varies over time and your first posts are pushed more than normal.

But in the current day, social media is a PVP zone: everybody fighting against each other for some reason. But it's a PVP zone where you can defend yourself by not engaging. So, what I do is simple: if I see an hateful comment, I'll ignore it, and if it's towards me I'll answer with something like "okay" followed by a stupid image or GIF (like the one you see below).

Yeah, I've seen the anime and it was amazing, 9/10. It's the reason I regained interest in playing a guitar, if we don't consider rhythm games. I have to do a rewatch some day.

How I'll use this blog

Considering this is my place where I can talk about anything, I'll simply write about stuff I do in my life that I want to share. It will be my own "safe zone" compared to the "PVP zone" that are social media.
I like to use these terms to describe some stuff, and I will use them. I'll leave a glossary for those in the future.

One day I might talk about a game I've played, another day about a book I've read or an album I've listened to. I can do anything and not care about numbers.
If you're interested, you can see what I'm up to here, and if you're not you can simply ignore this place.

Have a good day and remember to stay safe out there in the Internet Wastelands.
