Fortnite Made a Crazy Gacha

Retrieving date...

Fortnite made a crazy gacha with its chests and I'm participating for the fun of it.
I'm not even interested in this thing, except for playing Fortnite Festival, but here I am.

I'm not a fan of battle royales and multiplayer competitive games, but I still look into some of them. One is Fortnite, the other one is Rocket League.
I began playing Fortnite in 2017 during the pre-season, liked it and kept playing until Season 9. Now I usually play in the first season of every chapter, always in Zero Build because playing with builds is impossible for casual players like me, and if the game is balanced and fun to play I keep playing it.
Also, I play as Hatsune Miku. Because I have no other silly skins and I don't like being serious in competitive games.

This mini-season of Chapter 7 (whatever that means, we've clearly lost the plot) I began playing more because I'm having fun for once, but also for a cool thing they added.
Since this season is themed around One Piece, they've added the Pirate Token, which gives you free items from the shop for 14 FULL DAYS. Basically you're skipping the microtransaction part of a live service game. That's... a big deal.

The problem is that the odds are very low. One in 137.500 chances to find it, it's basically a lottery. ...I was actually expecting it to be rarer, I don't know why people are complaining for this. It's already a miracle this exists on a game that survives on microtransactions.
And the odds are not even that accurate because you need to find the right chest before the other players: the token spawns in one chest (if it does) at the start of the game, it's not rolling everytime you open a chest.

Now, on your typical, fair gacha, you have a pity system: after some pulls, you're guaranteed to get the reward you're looking for so that you don't spend too much money and lose your fanbase. For example, Project SEKAI has some sort of pity of 100 pulls on some banners, and Honkai Star Rail (the one I play the most) has a pity of 160 pulls. Enough pulls to not be a problem for my and the devs' pockets.
On unfair gachas, you don't have that. Pokémon TCG Pocket is one of them: you simply know you have low chances of finding that card, but never if and when you'll find it.

Fortnite made something worse though: it's a limited reward for this month only and it's clearly part of a retention program or to create fomo. This is why I don't care about it that much. If I find it, okay, I can get Kasane Teto for free, but if I don't it's not a big deal, I'll continue living my life as usual.
It's also because I'm only interested in jam tracks for Fortnite Festival, which is a gamemode that will certainly be discontinued the next year. I will FC Through the Fire and Flames in Guitar before that happens, but that's another story. I FC'd M@GICAL☆CURE! LOVE ♥ SHOT! in the meantime and it was stressful. Those patterns are crazy.

Remember that you're gambling for cosmetics in a live service game and not gaining actual money. And that Rock Band 4 died for Fortnite Festival.
