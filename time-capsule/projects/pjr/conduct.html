Introduction

You may know about the concept of fair use. That's the big, gray area I'm in with this project.
And in case you're wondering, yeah: there's something similar to fair use in Italy, that are, in short terms, called "exceptions to copyright law". I made sure to check them and they're slightly strictier than the fair use you may know.

So, I put these rules in place for myself to limit the damage I may cause and make them cancel out with the benefits I may give.
Or, at least, I try to do so.

Project R Code of Conduct

These six rules will sound like I'm trying to excuse myself, but that's what I always did and I thought if would be okay to publicly share this code I follow since 2020 for being 100% transparent with this.

Some of this rules are in place for following fair use, while others are there for the exceptions to copyright law.

1. I earn no money from this.

The golden rule of fair use: use copyrighted content for educational purposes and without gaining money.
That's because most of the songs I like, have charted and will chart are or may be copyrighted, so it'd be wise to not earn money off of it. The only thing I'm doing is creating something on top of it.
I don't earn money from the downloads and any chart request will always be free of charge.

2. I use the track only for charting.

If I happen to get a track by force because there's no downloads available (you know what I mean by that), that audio will only be used in charting sessions.
When I'm not charting, I'm listening that on streaming services, radio stations, physical media... you name it.

3. I don't chart new songs.

I'm not charting songs that released in the last year if they can generate revenue.
It sounds stupid, but for me... it feels wrong to chart a song when it's in this period.

For example, if something came out the 26th of April 2026, I won't chart it until the 26th of April 2027.

4. I always give full credit.

Full credits are always given to the artist, and links to their socials and places where to listen to the song are given whenever possible.
They deserve recognition for their work. Again, I'm just adding toppings to the pizza they created.

5. I'm not responsible for misusage of the downloaded content.

I have no idea what the people will do with the things they download and I'm not responsible for it. This stuff is intended to be used only on rhythm games and nowhere else, and it will always be like this.
I wish there was an easier way to prevent this.

6. I'm listening to the artists' requests.

While navigating in this gray area, I'm always trying to respect the work of others while not bothering you with my little things.
If I missed something (for example, you want a link or a copyright notice inside the chart), you can tell me and I'll fix it.
If you want a chart removed with one of your songs... it sucks, but I'll do it without complaining.
