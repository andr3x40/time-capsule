Devlog 4 - Implementing Aero

2026-10-08

After a quick research on Wikipedia, I learned that the Aero style is characterised by bright colours and skeuomorphism.

That second box was already checked because I was creating some glassy textures, but I was missing the first one.
You see... I like a dark theme for my website. But... yeah, Aero was definetely a light theme. So, I adapted the entire website's colours to support both.

Since I spend a lot of time in front of a screen because every single thing I like to do involves a computer of some sort, I love having a dark theme that doesn't fry my retinas even more.

Of course, I tried to give my own spin to an Aero theme and created this beauty that works even in the dark, but I feel I need to create a test page to see all of my components in action.

With theming crossed from my list of things, what's left now is:

  • setting up an RSS feed for blog and devlogs
  • creating the Music page for my playlists
  • filling up the list of Clone Hero charts
  • creating the Luna theme
