Devlog 7 - But Atom is Even Better

2026-10-19

After doing my RSS feeds, I discovered about Atom. Which is RSS but with some quality-of-life changes.
...I should do better researches before implementing something.

The only notable differences between RSS and Atom are clearer tags and — I kid you not, better timestamps. They use a better standard than RSS's.
I can decide whether to put the entire post or a part of it without making it confusing and add an update time to a post that's different than the time of publication.
I like having these extra things, okay?

Another thing I had to fix was the blog structure itself: all posts were crammed in a single page and it messes up with RSS readers because they don't know where to look for the full article.
Luckily, I only had to move some stuff to new pages and create a component for the sidebar so that I don't have to copypaste it every single time.

For the devlogs, though, I had to do something different: considering these posts are not that meaningful and they only serve as a documentation of my pain and suffering, I decided to have the index page summarise all the versions instead of showing a preview of every log.

And with this, I can finally say that I'm done with the blog part and I can start posting my random things.
The language thing will come after. A translator can do the job for now, even if it's not accurate most of the times.
